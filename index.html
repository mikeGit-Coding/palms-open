The Voice Behind The Mic

Palms open means you stop gripping.

I’m Melissa, and I created Palms Open because when I was in the thick of single motherhood, I didn't need another curated influencer telling me to take a bubble bath. I needed real answers to terrifying questions: How do I feed my family on one paycheck? How do I stop crying in the pantry after the kids fall asleep? How do I build a future when I feel completely alone?

Every single week on this show, I sit down with an expert who knows the data and a mom who has lived the battle. Together, we unpack one thing single mothers carry—the 2 a.m. guilt, the custody paperwork, the quiet holidays. And every conversation ends with one practical tool you can use this week, in ten minutes, for free.
